Guided check

Clean, or magnetized?

Set a baseline against the ambient field, then hold your watch up to the back of your iPhone. Magnetick tells you, in plain words, whether the case is pulling on the field around it.

Normal

Your watch is in the clear

The reading sits within a few percent of the ambient baseline — no measurable magnetism on the case.

Affected

Time for a trip to the demagnetizer

A clear deviation means the case is magnetized — a likely suspect when a mechanical watch starts running fast.

Built-in guide

Know where to hold the watch.

The magnetometer sits in a different spot on every iPhone — and a few centimeters off can mean a missed reading. Magnetick detects your model and points to the exact place to rest the watch, sourced from public iFixit teardowns.

  • Auto-detects your iPhone model
  • An on-screen overlay shows where to rest the caseback
  • Searchable index covering every supported iPhone

The whole app

One job. Done well.

Live μT readings

Taps your iPhone's built-in magnetometer through Core Motion and reports field strength in microtesla — straight from the sensor.

30-second chart

A rolling chart traces the last 30 seconds, so you can see exactly how the field changes as you bring the watch closer.

Stable-reading hint

Magnetick highlights when the signal settles, making it easier to tell a steady value from environmental noise.

Fully offline

No network requests. Use it on the bench, on a plane, or in a watchmaker's back room — works without a connection.

No data collection

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers. Readings never leave your device.

Honest about limits

A hobbyist tool, not a calibrated instrument. Perfect for spotting a magnetized watch — not for safety-critical work.
