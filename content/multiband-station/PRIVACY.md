# Privacy Policy

**App:** Multiband Station
**Developer:** Hieu Pham
**Contact:** hieupv.global@gmail.com
**Effective date:** November 17, 2026

## Summary

Multiband Station does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

## Information we collect

**None.** Multiband Station does not collect any personal information, device identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports, or any other data about you or your device.

## Permissions

Multiband Station does not request any system permissions. It does not access your microphone, location, contacts, photos, or notifications. The app only plays audio through your iPhone's speaker or wired earphones.

To pick the right time-signal station and encode the current time, the app reads your iPhone's time zone setting and system clock. This information is:

- Read on your device only.
- **Never** stored.
- **Never** transmitted off the device.

## Network access

Multiband Station does not make any network requests. The app functions fully offline and does not need an internet connection.

## Third parties

Multiband Station does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect data. There are no advertising networks, no analytics providers, and no crash-reporting services bundled in the app.

## Children's privacy

Multiband Station is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your rights

Because no data is collected, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in app updates.

## Contact
