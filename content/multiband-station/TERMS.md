## 2. Intended use

Multiband Station is a consumer convenience tool. It turns your iPhone's speaker or wired earphones into a short-range emulator of the long-wave time-signal stations (JJY 40/60, WWVB, MSF, DCF77, BPC) so that a radio-controlled watch held right next to the iPhone can sync its time.

The App is **not** an official, certified, or calibrated time source and is **not** intended for:

- Medical, diagnostic, or safety-critical use of any kind.
- Legal, regulatory, or other official timekeeping, or any situation where an authoritative time reference is required.
- Industrial, navigational, or professional measurement.
- Any decision where an inaccurate output could result in injury, financial loss, or property damage.

**Hearing safety.** To reach the watch, the App plays a loud, sometimes high-pitched tone at full volume. Never wear earphones or earbuds while the App is transmitting. If you use wired earphones, keep them away from your ears and rest an earbud next to the watch instead.

Reception depends on your iPhone's audio hardware, the watch model, the position of the watch relative to the speaker, and electromagnetic interference in the surrounding environment. A successful sync is not guaranteed.
