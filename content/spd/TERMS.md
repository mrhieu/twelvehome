## 2. Intended use

Seconds per day is a consumer tool that helps watch enthusiasts log and track the accuracy of their mechanical watches over time, expressed as seconds-per-day deviation against a reference time. Watch entries and measurement history you record can be synced across your devices through your account.

The App is **not** a calibrated scientific instrument and is **not** intended for:

- Industrial, navigational, or professional measurement.
- Any decision where an inaccurate output could result in injury, financial loss, or property damage.

Accuracy figures depend on the reference time source you compare against, the precision of your inputs, and the behaviour of the watch itself. Treat readings as approximate.
