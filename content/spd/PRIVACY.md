# Privacy Policy

**App:** Seconds per day
**Developer:** Hieu Pham
**Contact:** hieupv.global@gmail.com
**Effective date:** May 9, 2026

## Summary

Seconds per day is a watch-accuracy tracking app for iOS. To sync your watch entries and measurement history across your devices, the app uses a user account and a hosted backend. The app also uses analytics SDKs to understand aggregate usage. This policy explains what is collected, why, and how it is handled.

## Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

- **Watch entries you create** — names, brands, models, and any photos or notes you attach to a watch in the app.
- **Measurement history** — the timestamps and accuracy readings (seconds per day deviation) you record against each watch.
- **Account information** — the identifier you sign in with (e.g. an email address, or the anonymized identifier returned by Sign in with Apple) so your data can be associated with your account and synced across your devices.
- **Device identifiers and analytics** — anonymized device and usage identifiers, app session events, performance data, and crash diagnostics, used in aggregate to understand how the app is used and to fix bugs.

## Network access

Seconds per day makes network requests to the following destinations:

- **Our backend, hosted on Supabase**, to authenticate your account and sync your watch entries and measurement history across the devices where you are signed in.
- **Analytics providers** (see "Third parties" below) to send aggregate usage and diagnostic events.

The app does not require an internet connection for local entry of measurements, but sync and account features need network access.

## Third parties

Seconds per day integrates the following third-party SDKs:

- **Supabase** — used to host your account and the watch entries / measurement history you sync. Data sent to Supabase is governed by Supabase's terms and privacy policy.
- **Amplitude** — used for product analytics. Receives anonymized usage events and device identifiers to help us understand how features are used in aggregate.
- **Google Firebase Analytics** — used for product analytics and crash/performance diagnostics. Receives anonymized usage and device data.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Children's privacy

Seconds per day is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the data associated with your account at any time by emailing **hieupv.global@gmail.com**. Deleting your account removes your synced watch entries and measurement history from our backend. Uninstalling the app removes any locally stored data from your device.
