How it works

A time-signal station in your pocket

Radio-controlled watches set themselves from long-wave time-signal stations. Multiband Station recreates that signal right next to the watch.

A tone at ⅓ or ⅕ of the frequency

The iPhone plays a tone at one third or one fifth of the station's carrier frequency.

Harmonics recreate the carrier

Harmonics from the iPhone's audio hardware land on the real 40–77.5 kHz carrier that the watch listens for.

The official time code

The carrier is keyed with the station's official time code, timed to your iPhone's network-synced clock.

Range: a few centimetres

The signal is very weak, so the watch has to sit right on the speaker. It does not reach across a room.

How to sync

Five steps

  1. 1

    Set the watch's Home City

    Pick a city served by the station you want to use. See the stations table below.

  2. 2

    Turn the iPhone volume all the way up

    Use the volume buttons to set the media volume to maximum.

  3. 3

    Place the watch on the speaker

    Lay the watch face-up on the iPhone's bottom speaker grille. With wired earphones, rest an earbud on the watch instead.

  4. 4

    Tap Start

    The station defaults to Auto, which is chosen from your time zone. You can also pick a station yourself.

  5. 5

    Start a manual receive on the watch

    Hold the receive button for about 2 seconds (check your watch manual for which button). Keep the watch and iPhone still for 2–10 minutes.

Hearing safety: the tone is loud and can be high-pitched. Never wear earphones or earbuds while the app is transmitting.

Troubleshooting

Not syncing?

The watch doesn't pick up the signal

Rotate the watch, or slide it slowly across the speaker grille. The watch's antenna only picks up the signal well in certain positions, and the best spot differs between watch and iPhone models.

Can I use wired earphones?

Yes, and they sometimes work better than the speaker. Rest one earbud on the watch. If the signal is still weak, turn off Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Headphone Safety › Reduce Loud Audio. Never wear the earphones while the app is transmitting.

It still fails for my station

Try the other station for your region: JJY 40 instead of JJY 60 (or the other way round) in Japan, or MSF instead of DCF77 (or the other way round) in Europe. Pick it in the app instead of Auto. One may come through better than the other on your iPhone.

Reception keeps failing at my desk

Chargers, laptops and induction cookers give off electrical noise that can drown out the signal. Move away from them, or try another room.

Does it work over Bluetooth or AirPlay?

No. The signal has to come from the iPhone's built-in speaker or wired earphones. Bluetooth headphones and speakers (including AirPods) and AirPlay won't work.
